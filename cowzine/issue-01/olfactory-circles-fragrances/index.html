Olfactory Circles fragrances

Inessa L · Printed pages 31

Olfactory Circles is a non-visual multimodal olfactory experience (olfactus meaning "the sense of smell") created by multimedia artists, designers, singers & performers: Ivan Isakov, Inessa Lomas, Andrey Novikov and Yana Vlaeva.

Custom fragrances created for this experience take you on a round trip through space, time, nature, culture, rituals and earth, evoking long lost memories and crafting new visions.

The scents are aided by soundscapes, ancient poetry and folk music.

Here we explore what each fragrance is made of and how they relate to each other.
